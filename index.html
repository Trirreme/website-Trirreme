The company

A technical team that stays until the system runs

Trirreme started from a simple observation: most companies already have the data they need, but can't turn it into decisions. We work on that bridge — from the raw number to the dashboard the board opens every morning, from the process on paper to the system the team actually uses.

We are a lean team of data, software and management. Written scope before we start, one person accountable for your project, and no stage handed off to someone who has never spoken to you.

We serve small and mid-sized companies, independent professionals and industrial operations in Porto Velho and across Brazil, remotely.
